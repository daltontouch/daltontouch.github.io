Featured case

School photography ordering

The studio had run photo day on paper envelopes for 15 years. Parents filled out packages, backdrops, and quantities by hand. Staff retyped every order into the print lab. Peak season meant late nights, misspelled names, and lost forms.

I built a parent-facing site so they can order and pay on their phone, plus admin tools that export a clean file the lab can ingest. Same business, no typing pool.
